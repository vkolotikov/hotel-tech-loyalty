Before you begin

Clear from day one.

Do I need lifting experience?

No. The assessment gives us a safe, useful starting point whether this is your first programme or your fiftieth.

Is this an open gym?

Foundry is appointment-led. Members train in coached private or semi-private sessions, so the floor stays calm.

Can you work around an injury?

Often, yes. Share your history before booking; where needed, we will coordinate with your clinician.
