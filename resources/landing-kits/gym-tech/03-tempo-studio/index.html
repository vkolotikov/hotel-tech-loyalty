Which session should I start with?

Build is a good first choice. Tell the coach you are new and they will guide your setup and options.

Do I need to be fit already?

No. Sessions have clear progressions and regressions, so you can train alongside the group at the right level.

What should I bring?

Training clothes, clean indoor shoes and water. Towels, lockers and all equipment are ready here.
