Before your table

The useful details.

Can you accommodate dietary requirements?

Yes. Please share them while reserving so the kitchen can prepare with care. A vegetarian Menu Vela is available with 48 hours’ notice.

Is there a dress code?

There is no formal code. We simply invite guests to dress for an evening they would like to remember.

What is the cancellation policy?

Reservations may be changed up to 24 hours ahead. The tasting menu and private salon require 48 hours’ notice.
