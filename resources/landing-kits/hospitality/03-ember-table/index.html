Can you accommodate dietary requirements?

Yes. Tell us when reserving and we will confirm what the kitchen can prepare. A vegetarian tasting menu is available with notice.

What is your cancellation policy?

Tables may be changed or cancelled up to 24 hours ahead. The counter menu requires 48 hours’ notice.

Do you welcome walk-ins?

Always, when space allows. The wine bar keeps several places unreserved each evening.
