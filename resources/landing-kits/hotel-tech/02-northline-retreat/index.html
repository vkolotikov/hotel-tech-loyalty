Before the road north.

How do we reach Northline?

We are 90 minutes from Riga by car. Direct-booking guests can request a private station transfer.

Is the sauna included?

Yes. The shared lakeside sauna is heated every evening; private sauna time can be reserved on arrival.

Can children stay?

Guests aged twelve and over are welcome in all suites. The North House welcomes families with younger children.
