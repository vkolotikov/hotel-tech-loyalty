Before you arrive

Good to know.

What time is check-in?

Rooms are ready from 15:00. If you arrive earlier, leave your bags and begin exploring; late self-arrival is always available.

Is breakfast included?

Yes, when booking directly. Breakfast is served from 08:00–11:00, with early takeaway available by request.

Can you arrange airport transfer?

Yes. Add your flight details when booking and we will confirm a private transfer.
