Before you arrive

A few useful things.

Choose the closest option when booking; your practitioner can adjust the details with you.

What should I bring?

Just yourself. Robes, towels, sandals and secure storage are provided. Bring swimwear if your ritual includes the thermal room.

How early should I arrive?

We recommend 20 minutes. It gives you time to settle, have tea and speak with your practitioner without shortening your treatment.

Can you adapt treatments?

Yes. Share preferences, sensitivities or access needs when booking and again on arrival. We will explain suitable adjustments.

What is the cancellation window?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice when possible. The current policy is always shown before you confirm a booking.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes. Choose a named ritual or an open value, delivered digitally or presented at the house.
