Before your visit

A few useful answers.

Choose the closest service when booking; we will confirm the right amount of time with you.

Which service should I choose? +

Choose the closest match and add a note when booking. For a major colour change, book a consultation first so we can plan timing and pricing together.

Do I need a colour consultation? +

Yes for corrections, transformations or if you have not visited us before for colour. Smaller gloss and tone appointments can usually be booked directly.

What is your cancellation policy? +

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice when changing an appointment. Late changes may require a booking fee before a new time can be reserved.

Can I bring reference images? +

Absolutely. A small set of references helps us understand the mood, shape and colour you are drawn to, even when your final look is different.

Is the studio accessible? +

The main studio is step-free from the street. Contact us before your visit if you would like help planning your arrival or have specific access needs.
