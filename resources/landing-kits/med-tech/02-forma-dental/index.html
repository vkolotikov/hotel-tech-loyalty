Good to know

Before your visit.

I feel nervous about dental visits. Can you help?

Yes. Tell us when booking. We can allow more explanation time, agree pauses and discuss comfort options before examination.

Will I receive prices before treatment?

Yes. After assessment, we provide a written estimate and explain which items are urgent, optional or suitable to phase.

Do you offer emergency appointments?

A limited number of urgent appointments are held each week. Call the studio so we can understand the problem and advise.
