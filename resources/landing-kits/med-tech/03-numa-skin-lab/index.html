Why is a consultation required?

Skin type, medical history, current products and recent exposure can affect suitability and settings. Assessment comes first.

How much downtime should I expect?

It varies by protocol and individual response. We explain the likely range and aftercare before you decide to proceed.

Can a specific result be guaranteed?

No. Response varies between patients. We set realistic goals, document progress and change course when appropriate.
