Do I need a consultation first?

Yes. Suitability, expected outcomes, alternatives and fees are discussed before any medical treatment is scheduled.

Will I be advised about downtime?

Yes. Your plan includes preparation, aftercare and the likely recovery window for the selected treatment.

Are results guaranteed?

No medical outcome can be guaranteed. Individual response varies, and your physician will explain realistic expectations.
