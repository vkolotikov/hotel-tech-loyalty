HexaTech Loyalty 2.1.0: calendar visibility, device lock and notification safeguards for the new members design.

Please check:
- Sign in with a member account; existing sessions remain supported.
- Home, Rewards, the central Card, Events and More: check icons and touch targets on iPhone and iPad. Events must remain in the dock even without upcoming events or attendance history.
- Card QR, membership dates, assigned benefits and programme/access details. Open balances for points, stamps and credit where configured.
- Redeem once after confirming the cost; the returned code should appear in the same popup. Open/save/cancel a private PDF/image voucher from Offers and Inbox.
- Register directly from Home or Events, confirm cancellation and view attended history. Check full/refused states and points-off memberships.
- Have staff add points, confirm a check-in or add a stamp using isolated data. The app should refresh and acknowledge the confirmed change once, without replaying old awards.
- Profile/birthday edits, Preferences, message switches, language, password and guarded account deletion. Check input labels with VoiceOver.
- Enable device lock, restart, and verify authentication is required. Open a popup, leave for over two minutes, return and cancel authentication: private screens/popups must stay hidden until unlocked.
- Switch device notifications off during connection/reconnection. Try offline then return online; the preference must remain actionable. Return from system notification settings and check the connection again.
- Popup Close/outside tap/Back and long-sheet drag dismissal; keyboard layout, larger text, screen reader and Reduce Motion. Pending writes must not submit twice or dismiss early.
- Wallet and push only where the venue/provider and physical device support them.

Please report your device/OS and the exact steps for any issue. Test the installed build on supported physical devices; browser/unit checks do not verify native sharing, camera, biometrics, Wallet or push. Appointment and accommodation booking remain outside this loyalty release.
