HexaTech Loyalty is the membership card of a venue that uses HexaTech. Sign in using the demo account supplied separately in the console's App Review Information / App access fields.

The demo account belongs to HexaTech App Review Demo. Home, Card, Rewards, Activity and More contain demo membership data. Rewards includes a demo welcome drink and a late-checkout benefit. The demo venue has no bookable accommodation or appointments. No real purchase is required. Payments, where enabled by a venue, are for real-world services, not digital content.

Camera permission is used only to scan QR codes. Face ID or fingerprint protection is optional and handled by the device.

This update replaces an older app using the same bundle ID and safely clears any old staff session so a member can sign in.
