2. Purpose and audience
HexaTech Loyalty is for members of customer venues using HexaTech. It puts their membership card, dates, benefits, points where enabled, rewards, personal offers and event registrations in one place, with venue branding. Staff administration is a separate app. Appointment/accommodation booking and payments are excluded from version 2.1.

4. External services
Membership, authentication and eligibility come from the HexaTech HTTPS API at app.hexa-tech.uk. Expo supplies build/push infrastructure; Android delivery uses Firebase/FCM and iOS uses APNs. Optional Apple Wallet and Google Wallet appear only when venue/provider/device support them. Private vouchers use account authorization and the operating system's save/share facilities at the member's request. No AI service or digital purchase is required for core functionality.

5. Regional behaviour
Membership functionality is consistent across regions. Benefits, events, rewards, currency, venue-local dates and Wallets depend on venue/provider configuration and member eligibility. English, Russian, German, French and Spanish are available. The app does not request location access.

6. Regulated services and content
The app provides general loyalty functions, not medical advice, regulated financial services or gambling. Venue content is account-scoped. The publisher is FDS Cards Ltd. No protected third-party entertainment catalogue is bundled. Any specific venue authorization requested by App Review must be supplied by the owner rather than inferred from demo data.
