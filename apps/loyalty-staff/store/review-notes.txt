HexaTech Staff is the desk app for staff at venues using HexaTech. A staff account is required; sign in using the demo account supplied separately in the console's App Review Information / App access fields.

The demo account belongs to HexaTech App Review Demo. Use Members to find App Review Member, or Scan > Type instead and enter HL-2026-000493. Award demo points, check the member in, and approve/complete the pending late-checkout benefit request. Requests also contains a pending demo welcome-drink reward redemption. No real purchases, guests or venue reservations are involved.

NFC requires a venue physical card; the camera reads a member QR code from HexaTech Loyalty. The app is intended for unlisted distribution; an unlisted distribution request will follow the review process.
