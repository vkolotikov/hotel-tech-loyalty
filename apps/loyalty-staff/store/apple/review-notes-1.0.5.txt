2. Purpose and audience
HexaTech Staff is a native desk tool for authorised employees of venues using HexaTech loyalty software. It identifies members, shows points/benefits, records visits and eligible purchases, awards/uses points, links loyalty cards and fulfils reward/benefit requests. It replaces manual desk lookups; the server enforces venue scope and staff permissions. It is separate from the consumer HexaTech Loyalty app. We intend unlisted distribution to participating venues' employees, including personal devices, and will request an unlisted link before release.

3. Access and main features
Use the staff demo credentials in the separate Sign-in Information fields. They access HexaTech App Review Demo only; no real purchase or customer data is needed. No invitation, location restriction or physical card is needed for the main workflows.
- Launch and sign in. Home shows desk activity and shortcuts.
- Open Members and find App Review Member / HL-2026-000493. Open the member for the card, benefits, points, history and linked cards.
- Give points or Record visit demonstrates a demo desk action. Record purchase records an amount for loyalty calculations; it does not take payment.
- Requests shows rewards and benefit requests, with pending and completed views. Pending demo items can be approved/declined and fulfilled.
- Scan > Number works without hardware. QR uses the camera; NFC requires a compatible physical venue card and NFC-capable iPhone. iPad uses QR or Number.
- Profile has language, optional biometric lock, alerts and sign out. More opens management pages in the system browser, with a separate web sign-in.
Staff accounts are created/removed by the employer's administrator outside this app. There is no self-registration. Data-removal requests: https://app.hexa-tech.uk/data-deletion. Notes and request reasons are private venue/member business records. There is no public posting, anonymous chat, public profile or user discovery; venue administrators control staff access.

4. External services and purchases
Our first-party HTTPS backend at app.hexa-tech.uk provides authentication, permissions, member data and loyalty decisions. Expo Push Service and Apple Push Notification service deliver optional alerts. Apple camera, Core NFC and LocalAuthentication provide scanning and device biometrics; biometric data stays on-device. No AI service, advertising network or payment processor is used for core desk functionality. The app is free and offers no in-app digital purchase, subscription or paid-content checkout. Venues arrange their business service separately.

5. Regions
Functionality is consistent across regions. English, German, Spanish, French and Russian are selectable. Currency/time zone, enabled loyalty features and permissions vary by venue configuration, not App Store territory.

6. Regulatory/content rights
This is loyalty administration, not a banking, gambling, healthcare or other regulated professional service. It uses the publisher's supplied HexaTech artwork and licensed software dependencies, with no protected third-party entertainment catalogue.
