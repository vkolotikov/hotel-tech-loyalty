Purpose and audience
HexaTech Staff is a native operational app for authorised employees of venues using the HexaTech loyalty platform. Staff identify members, inspect membership benefits, record visits and eligible purchases, award/use loyalty points, link loyalty cards, and fulfil reward/benefit requests. It replaces manual desk lookups and keeps actions under the venue's server-enforced permissions. It is not the consumer HexaTech Loyalty app or the older Hotel Staff hotel-operations app. We intend unlisted distribution to participating venues' employees, including their personal devices, and will request an unlisted link through Apple's process before release.

Access and walkthrough
Use the staff demo login in the separate Sign-in Information fields. It belongs only to HexaTech App Review Demo; no real customer data or purchases are involved. No registration, invitation, payment, location restriction or physical card is needed to review the main workflows.
1. Launch and sign in. Home shows desk activity and shortcuts.
2. Open Find and search for App Review Member or member number HL-2026-000493. Open the member to inspect points, benefits, history and cards.
3. From the member, Give points or Record visit demonstrates a desk action. Record purchase records a purchase amount for loyalty calculations; it does not charge a payment method. Changes affect only the isolated demo member.
4. Open Requests to inspect reward redemptions and benefit requests, including completed history. Available pending demo requests can be approved/declined and fulfilled.
5. Scan supports Number without hardware. QR uses the camera with a member's QR code. NFC requires a compatible physical venue card and an NFC-capable iPhone; it is optional and is not available on iPad. iPad users can use QR or Number.
6. Open the profile from Home for language, optional biometric lock, request alerts and sign out. More opens the venue's management pages in the system browser; these use a separate web sign-in.

Accounts and content
Staff accounts are created and removed by the employer's administrator outside this app. This app has no account-registration flow. Account/data-removal requests are available at https://app.hexa-tech.uk/data-deletion. Staff-entered transaction notes and request reasons are private business records, scoped to the venue and the relevant member, not a public social feed or anonymous chat. Venue administrators manage staff access; the app has no public posting, public profiles or user discovery.

External services and monetisation
The first-party HTTPS service at app.hexa-tech.uk provides staff authentication, permissions, member data and all loyalty decisions. Expo Push Service and Apple Push Notification service deliver optional staff request alerts. Native Apple camera/Core NFC and LocalAuthentication provide scanning and optional device biometrics; biometric data stays on the device. The app does not use an AI service, advertising network or payment processor for its core desk functionality. The app is free; no in-app digital purchases, subscriptions or paid-content checkout are offered. Venue administrators arrange their business service separately.

Regions and rights
The same app functionality is offered across regions. English, German, Spanish, French and Russian are selectable; venue currency/time zone, configured loyalty features and staff permissions may differ by venue, not by App Store territory. This is a loyalty administration tool, not banking, gambling, healthcare or another regulated professional service. It uses the publisher's supplied HexaTech artwork and licensed software dependencies, with no protected third-party entertainment catalogue.
