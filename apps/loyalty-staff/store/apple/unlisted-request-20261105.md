## Business problem and why unlisted helps

HexaTech Staff is a final native operational app for authorised employees of customer venues using the HexaTech loyalty platform. At a reception or service desk, staff scan or find a member, inspect their benefits and points, record a visit or eligible purchase, award or redeem loyalty points, link a card, and approve or fulfil reward/benefit requests. For example, reception staff can identify a member by a typed membership number when no NFC card is available, then fulfil an eligible late-checkout request. Authentication and server-side venue/role permissions restrict all business data. A direct installation link lets each participating venue onboard its authorised staff without marketing this staff-only tool to the general public. App 6829777674, version 1.0.5 build 3, was resubmitted to App Review on 5 November 2026 and is Waiting for Review. Initial rollout figures are planning estimates: 100 staff users across 50 customer businesses, with approximately 80 unmanaged/personal devices.

## Why unlisted instead of public discovery

The audience is employees of participating customer businesses, not general consumers searching the App Store. An employer creates the staff account; there is no public self-registration and an unauthorised person cannot use the desk features. Public discovery would confuse consumers looking for the separate HexaTech Loyalty member app. We will give the unlisted link to participating venue administrators for staff onboarding, while keeping normal App Store installation and updates.

## Why unlisted instead of private Apple Business distribution

Participating venues are separate businesses and staff may use their personal, unmanaged iPhones or iPads. These businesses and devices are not all enrolled in Apple Business or an MDM programme. Requiring every small venue to enrol and manage devices would prevent straightforward staff onboarding. One unlisted App Store link supports these customer employees across businesses and devices, while sign-in, venue scope and staff permissions enforce access.
