# Staff Home — Obsidian glass

The owner requested a more luxurious, creative Apple-like dark design, with glass, well-designed controls/icons, less copy and restrained animation. Confirm Home before designing the other screens. This is an editable design proposal, not a native implementation or store update.

## Design decisions

An iPhone-sized 393 × 852 screen. The venue mark and profile sit above Today. Scan is the signature action: an editable, angled membership-card object on a subdued alignment grid, with a champagne-metal arrow. Two compact tiles lead to Members and Requests; the latter carries the waiting count. One recent activity row gives context without turning Home into a report. Five labelled destinations remain in a floating glass dock.

SF Pro carries the iPhone controls and operational text; Cormorant Garamond is retained only for the venue wordmark. For Android implementation, use the platform-appropriate app typeface rather than embedding SF Pro. This intentionally follows the owner's dark/Apple-like brief instead of the house-style marketing default of alternating light surfaces.

Graphite `#080B10` / `#171C24`, white `#F6F2EA`, secondary text `#AAB1BE`, champagne `#D4BA83`. Gold is reserved for Scan, active navigation and attention. The floating card is vector artwork; there is no decorative stock photo or rasterized UI. Venue/member/activity data are fictional.

Glass is concentrated in navigation and small controls, with opaque content surfaces behind readable text. This follows [Apple's material guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials) and its advice to avoid piling glass surfaces over each other in [Meet Liquid Glass](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/). The Figma treatment is an approximation to validate composition, not a claim of native material performance on both platforms.

## Motion

- Arrival: short delay, then 600 ms ease-out as the card settles into its resting position.
- Scan touch study: 120 ms into the pressed state, 160 ms hold, then 280 ms ease-out back to rest. No looping shimmer or pulse.
- Reduced motion: show the resting state, without positional animation. Reduced transparency: solid graphite control surfaces. These are implementation requirements, not device settings wired into this prototype.
- Prototype uses Smart Animate between Home states. Connections and durations were read back. Playback has not been independently observed in the Figma presentation player; no camera, NFC or transaction is invoked.
