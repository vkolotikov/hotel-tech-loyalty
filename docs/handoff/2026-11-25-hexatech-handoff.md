# Handoff — 25 November 2026

## CRM email decisions and approved quotes

The lead assistant now supports a follow-up decision, opportunity-specific quote arithmetic, file metadata, task outcomes
and manager-reviewed wording. The response, proposal and follow-up workflows select the active brand’s knowledge by exact
technical reference and topic. Future decision dates, work owed by staff and contact preferences can produce a staff action
without opening a composer. Shared contacts with multiple opportunities require a project match for captured mail.

Two deidentified examples were run through the previous and updated real model workflows using the same business date.
The earlier workflow drafted a follow-up before the buyer’s board meeting. The new follow-up recommended waiting.
Both answered an indoor-only product question correctly, and the new reply explicitly retained the feasibility-only scope.
Automated coverage checks quote expiry, price rejection, past drafts, permissions, contact suppression and project isolation.
The [CRM runbook](../crm-workspace.md) covers entry points and the limits of external data.

## Shared company knowledge in CRM and chat

CRM proposals and responses retrieve live, brand-scoped chatbot answers, relevant document passages, the shared goal and private email instructions. The CRM reads customer correspondence and AI Discovery through the existing lead assistant. The mail app supplies the signature.

The brand selector in CRM setup and AI assistant knowledge uses the signed-in staff member’s available brands. The business wizard accepts existing chatbot knowledge as a source. Website capture uses GPT-6.2 Astra with high reasoning, follows sitemaps and nested links, and lists the public pages it read. Managers can review suggestions, edit the shared conversation goal and select individual company facts for visitor chat. Existing CRM fields become available to visitors when a manager selects them.
